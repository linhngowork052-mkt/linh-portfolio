Overview

The brief

Leisure Coast Fruit Market & Deli needed a way to turn local awareness into real engagement — and real engagement into foot traffic. My task was to build a complete digital communication plan: understand the brand and its competitors, define exactly who we were talking to, and design a channel and content strategy that could carry someone from first noticing the brand to becoming a loyal advocate.

I started with a brand and competitor audit, then used consumer behaviour insights to build a SMART-goal-based campaign mapped across the full customer journey on Facebook and Instagram — backed by persuasive sales page copy, retargeting ad concepts, and a 12-week content calendar built around fresh produce, community values, and seasonal promotions.

Meet the buyer persona

A local parent who values fresh, high-quality produce for her family and is drawn to brands that feel genuine and community-rooted. Every content theme and channel decision in the plan was built to speak directly to what Helen cares about — freshness, trust, and local connection.
