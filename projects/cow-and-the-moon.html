Social Media & Web Analytics: Cow and the Moon Gelato Bar

Overview

The brief

Cow and the Moon is an award-winning artisan gelato shop with a loyal following, but its digital presence was underleveraged — infrequent posting, minimal reply to comments, and a website that visitors left almost immediately. My task was to turn publicly available data into a clear picture of where the brand stood, how it compared to three local competitors (C9 Chocolate and Gelato, Dolce Dessert Bar, and Wendy's Milk Bar), and what it should do next.

I pulled together SWOT and PEST analysis, sentiment analysis across Instagram, Facebook and Google Reviews, social listening and netnography, and web analytics from Semrush and SimilarWeb — then benchmarked all of it against the competitor set before writing up strategic recommendations.

Analysis

SWOT snapshot

Strengths

  • High organic web traffic from effective SEO and specific branded keywords
  • Strong positive sentiment and brand loyalty across social platforms
  • High-quality, consistent visual content on Instagram

Weaknesses

  • Infrequent posting — around 3 posts a month
  • Minimal reply to comments, limiting perceived accessibility
  • High bounce rate (83.78%) and low pages-per-session (1.2)

Opportunities

  • Untapped potential on TikTok, a fast-growing short-form platform
  • Influencer and local advertiser collaborations
  • More behind-the-scenes and user-generated content

Threats

  • Crowded competitive landscape in Wollongong
  • Algorithm changes reducing organic reach
  • Low engagement risking visibility loss to more active competitors

Competitor Analysis

Where the gap is

C9 Chocolate and Gelato and Dolce Dessert Bar both post far more consistently, which showed up directly as an engagement advantage. Wendy's Milk Bar led on nearly every web metric — traffic, backlinks, time on page — backed by a much larger paid and SEO investment. Despite the smallest online footprint of the three challengers in some areas, Cow and the Moon still earned the highest likes-per-post of any brand studied, suggesting the loyalty is there — it just isn't being converted into consistent reach.

Recommendations

What I recommended

1

Invest in paid advertising

Move beyond full reliance on organic reach to unlock performance tracking and more predictable growth.

2

Improve website experience

Address the high bounce rate and low time-on-page with a more engaging, easier-to-navigate site.

3

Leverage AI

Use AI tools to personalise campaigns and improve marketing productivity and efficiency.

4

Develop strategic content marketing

Build a consistent content calendar, including user-generated content, to lift posting frequency and authenticity.

5

Expand keywords & SEO

Move beyond branded search terms to capture intent-based searches like "best gelato in Wollongong."

Takeaway

This project sharpened how I read digital performance data and turn it into a prioritised action plan — not just what a brand should do, but why, backed by sentiment, traffic, and competitor evidence.
