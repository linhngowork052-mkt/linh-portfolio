Overview

The brief

The assessment asked me to demonstrate digital marketing knowledge in a clear, practical way — so instead of writing another report, I built a structured tutorial video comparing Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads Manager, and Google Ads. The goal was to show how each platform actually works, not just describe it: audience targeting, campaign structure, creative formats, and performance tracking, backed by real platform visuals and examples.

Beyond the walkthrough, I wanted to show when each platform is the right choice — because knowing the interface means nothing without knowing the strategy behind it.

Platform Breakdown

Where each platform wins

Meta Ads Manager

Built for retargeting

Strong audience targeting and creative flexibility make Meta especially powerful for re-engaging people who already know the brand.

Strength: Retargeting & audience precision
TikTok Ads Manager

Built for creative engagement

Native, fast-moving creative formats give TikTok an edge for capturing attention and building engagement with Gen Z audiences specifically.

Strength: Creative reach with Gen Z
Google Ads

Built for intent

Search-based targeting captures people actively looking for a solution, making it the strongest platform for intent-driven conversion.

Strength: Intent-based search targeting
