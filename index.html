About

I used to see marketing as purely creative — good ideas, good content, put it out into the world. A few classes into my degree, I realised there's a whole system behind that content: decisions shaped by customer insight, by data, by actual need — not just what looks good. That changed how I saw the work. It stopped being something I just enjoyed, and became something I wanted to properly understand.

That shift became real at my internship at Tynan Motors, coordinating content across several automotive brands at once. I went in expecting to just schedule posts. Instead, I ended up researching competitors, scripting short-form video, and watching engagement numbers shift in response to decisions I'd actually made. It was the first time I saw that system in action — the insight, the decision, the content, the result — instead of just reading about it in a lecture slide.

"Behind every idea I ship, there's a decision I can explain."

People who've worked with me tend to describe me the same way: dependable — someone they can hand something to and trust it'll get done properly. Outside of work, I'm usually planning the next café to try, chasing good food, picking up a new skill just to see if I can, or saving up for the next trip.
